Contact Global Ski Atlas

Global Ski Atlas is a solo project built to help skiers compare downhill resorts with consistent OpenStreetMap-based data. Use this page to report missing or incorrect resort geometry, ask how a tool works, suggest blog topics, or send general feedback.

How we use your message

Messages go to the project maintainer through a contact form. We use the details you share only to respond and to improve the atlas. If you are reporting a data issue, include the resort name, approximate location, and what looks wrong on the interactive map or in Resort Facts. Many map fixes ultimately belong in OpenStreetMap; when that is the case we will point you to our OSM tagging guide.

Before you write

  • Browse the FAQ for common questions about the map, comparison tools, and coffee table book.
  • Read the About page for how the site and data pipeline are built.
  • Check the blog for guides on stats, passes, road trips, and open ski data.
  • Review the privacy policy for how analytics and advertising cookies work on this site.
