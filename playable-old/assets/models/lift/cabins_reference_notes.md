# Cabin references

Viewed 2026-10-21. Not copied into the mesh. Game sizes override the photos.

## Gondola analogue

Voss Gondol boarding platform, Wolfmann, CC BY-SA 4.0:

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Voss_Gondol_Aerial_gondola_passenger_lift_(taubane)_Cableway_cabins_and_platform_for_boarding_embarking_at_the_summit_station_top_terminal_at_Hanguren_Hangurstoppen_mountain_820_m_etc_Voss_Norway_2019-11-20_DSC00602.jpg

Angle: inside the terminal, side doors. People are about the door height.

Observed: rounded roof cap, dark framed glass doors on the long side, solid lower body, cabin about one person wide at the door. The hanger is not in frame. Doppelmayr OMEGA V published figures (not a photo): cabin length 1.94 m, height 2.22 m, hanger visually tucked into the roof. Those real sizes are larger than the game gondola.

## Cable-car analogues

Aeri de Montserrat, single car. Side three-quarter, CC. Local copy was only for inspection.

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aeri_de_Montserrat,_single_car.jpg

Observed: one heavy vertical hanger from a carriage on the cables down to the roof, roof rail, window band in the upper half, solid lower body, faceted corners, hanger thickness is a real fraction of the cabin width.

Peak 2 Peak carrier. This is a large 3S gondola, used only as a window-band and roof-round analogue, not as the cable car.

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Peak_2_Peak_Carrier.JPG

Observed: continuous dark window band over a solid skirt, rounded roof corners, hanger meets the roof center.

## Game frame

- Gondola body about 2.2 m wide, 1.8 m tall, 1.8 m deep, short hanger.
- Cable car body about 3.2 m wide, 2.2 m tall, 2.2 m deep, heavier hanger.
- Paint `0x6b7785`, darker glass in the same albedo, metalness 0.
- Origin at the grip. Front is Blender -Y so the exported file faces +Z.
- No cable, tower, ground, or people in the GLB.

## Assumptions

- Both vehicles are symmetric. Gondola doors are on the sides (±X). The cable car keeps a big window on the travel ends because it shuttles and does not turn around.
- The gondola roof is the round one. The cable car roof is flatter, with a rail and a yoke, so the silhouette differs before the size does.
