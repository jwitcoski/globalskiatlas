# Slalom gate references

Structure only. Nothing from these photos is copied onto the mesh. No logos.

## Liski C6 World Cup, product front

- Page: https://liski.us/liski-c6-world-cup-gate-w-arrow-28-base/
- Image viewed locally then removed (catalog photo, not for redistribution): two poles, red and blue, side by side.
- Upright is a uniform tube, about 30 mm, 180 cm above the snow. Cap is a short rounded plug the same diameter as the shaft, not a ball.
- A thin bright collar sits under the colored shaft.
- The hinge housing is a brighter green cylinder, a bit wider than the shaft, with the spring just above it.
- The base is a coarse screw (auger) narrower than the housing, meant to be buried. The housing is what meets the snow.
- No cloth panel on this pole. FIS slalom gates are the poles themselves. A gate is the pair, 4–6 m apart in a race. This game uses 8 m so the gap stays obvious inside the existing 10 m checkpoint circle.

## FIS flex-pole spec, November 2020

- https://assets.fis-ski.com/f/252177/fc0f441715/specifications_for_-flex_-poles_alpine_courses_nov-2020.pdf
- Upright above snow at least 1800 mm. Type A diameter 29–32 mm. Flex zone including the transition at most 300 mm long and 35 mm across. Top must be sealed and rounded, and must not be wider than the shaft.

## What the game changes on purpose

- Shaft diameter 72 mm. A 30 mm tube disappears at the distance the old ring was read.
- A small flag on the inner face of each pole, near the top, so the checkpoint still reads as a marker. It does not connect the poles.
- Both poles take the same runtime color. Race courses alternate red and blue. Here the color is the checkpoint state.
