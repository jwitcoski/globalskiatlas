<h2>Step 1. Size from trail counts</h2>
<p>Small / medium / large / mega is the plate used on this site. Most mapped hills are small. Destination weeks live in large and mega. A first trip with kids usually wants medium or small plus carpets. A week in France can eat mega. The chart is the file, not a taste ranking.</p>

<h2>Step 4. Pass last</h2>
<p>Indy, Epic, and Ikon cover a slice of the file. Most rows have no pass tag. If you already own a pass, filter with <a href="../wiki/browse.html">the wiki</a> and the pass overlay.
